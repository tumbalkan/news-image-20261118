Middle East — Gulf Crisis

Iran Fires Drones at US Bases in Bahrain and Kuwait

Iran fired drones at US bases in Bahrain and Kuwait on Wednesday in retaliation for American strikes on a Revolutionary Guard missile site on Qeshm Island. Bahrain intercepted the attacks. The exchange marks the sharpest escalation in the Gulf in years, with both sides signalling further action.
